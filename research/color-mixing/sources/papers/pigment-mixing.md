Primary-source excerpt (author page abstract, 21 words):

> We achieve this by establishing a latent color space, where RGB colors are represented as mixtures of primary pigments together with additive residuals.

Recorded facts: ordinary RGB represents colored-light mixing, whereas pigment appearance is modeled with Kubelka-Munk absorption/scattering. The paper makes an RGB-in/RGB-out approximation practical via a latent representation. It is a display/color-model result, not a fluid mass-transport model.
