Primary-source excerpt (abstract, 17 words):

> direct numerical simulations for Schmidt numbers between 1 and 64

Corroborating resolution study: Donzis et al., *Resolution effects and scaling in numerical simulations of passive scalar mixing in turbulence*, Physica D 239 (2010), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physd.2009.09.024. It reports that high-Schmidt scalar fields impose stricter resolution requirements through the Batchelor scale than the velocity field.

Recorded implication: a graphics grid cannot honestly claim molecularly resolved mixing at realistic high Schmidt number unless it resolves the smaller scalar scale. An explicit effective/subgrid diffusivity is an artistic model and should be named as such.
