Primary-source excerpt (transmittance chapter, 18 words):

> The beam transmittance Tr ... is the fraction of radiance transmitted between two points in a scene.

Recorded facts: homogeneous-medium transmittance is exponential in extinction and distance; absorption and scattering coefficients are nonnegative and have inverse-length units. This supports concentration-to-extinction mapping for clear dyed liquid.
