Primary-source excerpt (definition, 19 words):

> Proportionality constant D, relating the flux of amount of entities B to their concentration gradient Jn = -D grad cB.

Recorded facts: diffusivity has dimensions of area per time. For constant density and diffusivity, combining this constitutive relation with species continuity gives the familiar advection-diffusion equation used in the report.
