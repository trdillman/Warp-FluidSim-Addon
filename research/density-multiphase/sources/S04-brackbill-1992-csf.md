Narrow primary-source clip: interfacial tension is converted to a volume force across a finite-thickness color transition.

Evidence extracted:

- The force density is proportional to curvature and approaches the sharp interfacial force as interface thickness relative to radius tends to zero.
- The formulation avoids explicit surface connectivity and permits topology change.

Supported claims: CSF is straightforward on Eulerian grids, but curvature error, parasitic currents, and explicit capillary time-step restrictions require validation.
