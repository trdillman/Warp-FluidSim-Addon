Narrow primary-source clip: particles retain a locally affine velocity field during grid transfers.

Evidence extracted:

- The transfers conserve linear and angular momentum.
- APIC avoids PIC's strong dissipation and reduces FLIP transfer noise.

Supported claims: APIC is a strong transport basis for all phases, but it is not an interface-capturing or immiscibility method by itself.
